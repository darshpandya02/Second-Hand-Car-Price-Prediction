Used Car Price Estimator

Estimate the resale price of a used car in India. The model is a gradient-boosted tree ensemble trained on 6,926 de-duplicated CarDekho listings (model years 1983 to 2020) and runs entirely in your browser.

    Model and limits

    • Prices are CarDekho asking prices (INR) from a public Kaggle dataset last updated in January 2023; the newest model year is 2020. Prices today will differ.
    • The 90% interval comes from conformalized quantile regression; on held-out listings it covered about 90% of true prices. It is a statistical range, not a guarantee.
    • Luxury and rare models have few listings; the model tends to under-predict very expensive cars.
    • Condition, accident history, city and variant trim are not in the data.
